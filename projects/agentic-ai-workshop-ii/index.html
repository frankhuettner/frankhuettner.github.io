Agentic AI in Business, Workshop II

Students build a mail AI assistant for 3,244 real Enron mails, with Langflow and n8n on kingo-pod. Homework, workbooks and downloads.

What the students build

In Workshop II (9, 16 and 23 November 2026), students build an AI assistant for a real mailbox: 3,244 mails of Vince Kaminski, head of research at Enron in 2001. The assistant reads new mail, sorts it, and drafts answers in Kaminski’s words. It sends nothing without the student’s OK.

Langflow thinks, n8n does the work, and the student says OK. Everything runs on the student’s laptop, with kingo-pod.

The pages

  • Homework: three parts to get ready, with the test mailbox, Langflow and n8n.
  • 9 November: the workbook of the first morning, block by block as on the slides.
  • Homework until 16 November: Claude Code in a sandbox and the kimchi mail, and, if you like, an SMS from n8n.
