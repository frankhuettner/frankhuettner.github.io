Agentic AI in Business, Workshop II

Students build a mail AI assistant for 3,244 real Enron mails, with Langflow and n8n on kingo-pod. Homework, workbooks and downloads.

What the students build

In Workshop II (9, 16 and 23 November 2026), students build an AI assistant for a real mailbox: 3,244 mails of Vince Kaminski, head of research at Enron in 2001. The assistant reads new mail, sorts it, and drafts answers in Kaminski’s words. It sends nothing without the student’s OK.

Langflow thinks, n8n does the work, and the student says OK. Everything runs on the student’s laptop, with kingo-pod.

The pages

  • Homework: three parts to get ready, with the test mailbox, Langflow and n8n.
  • 9 November: the workbook of the first morning, block by block as on the slides.
  • Homework until 16 November: Claude Code in a sandbox and the kimchi mail, and, if you like, an SMS from n8n.
  • 16 November: the workbook of the second morning, block by block: a simple webhook, a page that asks the mailbox, a better search flow, the team’s rules. For the afternoon, one page per team: A · KSMC, B · Kgoda, C · K-Motor. And how to change the model that Claude Code uses.
  • Homework until 23 November: get more out of Claude Code, with nine habits and a small website of your own. It also works without the course’s setup.
  • 23 November: the workbook of the third morning, task by task: measure your pass rate with the test bench, and decide on whose computers your system runs. Optional: let an AI judge free text.

Downloads

  • ws2_hw.zip: the homework as one HTML file, the Langflow flows and the first n8n workflow.
  • ws2_s1.zip: the workbook and the slides of 9 November, the n8n workflows and the two CSV files for the data tables.
  • ws2_hw2.zip: the homework until 16 November as one HTML file, and the rules page for the team’s coding agent.
  • ws2_s2.zip: the workbook, the slides and the team pages of 16 November, with the n8n workflows, Langflow flows and data tables of the afternoon.
  • ws2_s3.zip: the workbook of 23 November, the test bench with its test mails (five for Triage, five for Draft Reply with the judge), and the two Triage flows.
