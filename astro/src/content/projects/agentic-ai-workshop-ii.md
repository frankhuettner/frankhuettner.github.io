## What the students build

In Workshop II (9, 16 and 23 November 2026), students build an AI assistant for a real mailbox: 3,244 mails of Vince
Kaminski, head of research at Enron in 2001. The assistant reads new mail, sorts it, and drafts answers in Kaminski's
words. It sends nothing without the student's OK.

Langflow thinks, n8n does the work, and the student says OK. Everything runs on the student's laptop, with
[kingo-pod](https://huettner.io/kingo-pod/).
