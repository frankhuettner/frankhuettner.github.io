I am Assistant Professor of Decision Sciences at
[SKK GSB](https://gsb.skku.edu/en/Faculty_Research/GSB_Faculty.do?mode=view&perId=LZStrC4BQkgpgjg8gigDwEoFcUE4BSBnA5gGwDUAnAVgHUBZXAXhqA%20&),
Sungkyunkwan University, in Seoul.

My research is in cooperative game theory and decision making. Much of it concerns
the **Shapley value** — its axiomatic foundations, its extensions to games with
externalities and to networks, and its use for decomposing goodness of fit. I also
work on **rational inattention** and consumer choice under limited attention, and
on **operations** problems such as the newsvendor model.

I am a member of the editorial board of _Mathematical Social Sciences_.

You can find my email address in my papers.
