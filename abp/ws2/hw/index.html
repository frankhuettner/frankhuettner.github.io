What you will build in Workshop II

In Workshop II (9, 16 and 23 November), you will build an AI assistant for a real mailbox.

  • The mailbox: 3,244 real mails of Vince Kaminski. He was head of research at Enron in 2001.
  • Where the mails live: in a new Gmail account, only for the course. We call it your test Gmail account.
  • What the AI assistant does: it reads new mail, sorts it, and drafts answers in Kaminski’s words. It sends nothing without your OK.

Your goal for 9 November

At the end of the first morning, this works on your laptop:

  1. You will write a mail from your SKKU mail account to your test Gmail account.
  2. Your AI assistant will sort the mail and draft Kaminski’s answer.
  3. You will read the draft and say OK.
  4. The answer will arrive in your SKKU inbox.

So you work with two mail accounts: your SKKU mail account, which you already have, and a new test Gmail account, which you make in step 2.

Two ideas you know from Yanbo's workshop
  • Decomposition. Your AI assistant is not one big AI that does everything. The job is cut into small steps, and each step has one task: n8n picks up the mail, Triage only sorts it, Draft Reply only writes the answer, n8n checks the rules, and you decide. Small steps are easy to test and to fix. On 9 November you will test Triage on its own first.
  • Guardrails. Every step is checked before the next one acts. n8n looks at each new mail first: it hides personal numbers, and a mail that tries to trick your AI assistant goes to you instead. Langflow checks its own answer: in step 8 you will see "checks_ok": true. And nothing leaves without your OK.
