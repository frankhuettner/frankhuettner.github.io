14. Look into the database with CloudBeaver

This part is optional: skip it if you like. CloudBeaver shows kingo-pod’s database in your browser: http://localhost:8978. In Workshop II, it shows you what your AI assistant did, row by row.

First time in CloudBeaver? Open Using CloudBeaver first and do its steps 1 and 2. This page is not just extra help: without it, you cannot get in. CloudBeaver has two logins, one for CloudBeaver and one for the database, with different passwords. The page shows both. You do it only once.

  1. On the left, open Classroom (PostgreSQL) → Databases → classroom → Schemas → n8n_workshop_mail → Tables.
  2. Double-click the table messages, then click the tab Data.
  3. One row is one mail of Kaminski’s mailbox. Scroll to the right: who sent it (from_addr), to whom (to_addrs), the subject (subject), the text (body_text).
  4. Find your test mail from step 12: sort the table by the column sent_at, newest first. To sort, move the mouse over the head of the column sent_at: small arrows appear. Click them until the newest mail is at the top. Your mail is the first row.

On 9 November, your n8n workflows work with exactly these rows.
