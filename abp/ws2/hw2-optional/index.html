Homework until 16 Nov, optional: Get ready for SMS

An SMS lets your system reach a person who is not at the laptop, for example the person who approves a draft. You need an account with a company that sends SMS, for example Twilio (or SOLAPI). The steps here use Twilio.

Twilio costs money. Its free trial account is enough for the two test SMS here, and it needs no credit card: it has 100 SMS, ends after 30 days, and sends only Twilio’s sample texts, not your own.

An SMS is not a safe channel. Never send a name or the text of a mail, even with a paid account. An SMS passes through the SMS company and the phone networks, and it can show up on a locked phone. Send only a short note, such as “A draft waits for you”.

11. Make a free Twilio trial account

  1. Open https://www.twilio.com/try-twilio and sign up with your SKKU address.
  2. Confirm your address with the mail Twilio sends you.
  3. Twilio asks for your mobile number and sends it a code. Type the code in.
  4. Send yourself a first SMS on Twilio’s website. This also makes your number a verified tester: a trial account sends SMS only to verified numbers.
    • On the first page of your Twilio account, under Try it out, tab Communications, click Send an SMS. The page Try out SMS opens.
    • Under To, open the list. Your number is there, with +82 in front and without the first 0 (010-1234-5678 becomes +821012345678)? Pick it. If not, pick Add a verified tester, type your number like that, and type in the code Twilio sends you.
    • Click Send SMS. Your phone shows it within a minute: Twilio works. Keep the SMS: you need the number it comes from in step 12.

Stuck? Help for step 11

12. Copy three things from Twilio into a note

  1. On the first page of your Twilio account (Home in the menu on the left), find the box with the name of your account, at first My First Twilio Account. Next to Account SID, click the copy icon, and paste it into a note.
  2. Next to Auth Token, click the copy icon too, and paste it into the same note. The token stays hidden as dots; the eye shows it.
  3. Add the number the SMS of step 11 came from, as a + and then only digits: +1 (737) 250-8034 becomes +17372508034. A trial account has no number of its own: it sends from numbers that Twilio shares among many accounts.

13. Give n8n the Twilio login: a credential

  1. In n8n, click the + at the very top left, then New credential.
  2. Type Twilio into the search box, pick Twilio API, and click Continue.
  3. Top left, name it Twilio.
  4. Paste the Account SID and the Auth Token from step 12. Click Save.

Stuck? Help for step 13

14. Send yourself an SMS from n8n

Build a small n8n workflow with two nodes:

  1. Make a new workflow and name it WS2.4 - TEST - SMS: a test, as the rules page names tests.
  2. Add a first node: the trigger Trigger manually. Name it trigger_manual.
  3. Add a Twilio node, the action Send an SMS/MMS/WhatsApp message. Name it send_sms.
    • Credential: Twilio
    • From: the number from step 12, such as +17372508034
    • To: your own mobile number with +82 in front and without the first 0: 010-1234-5678 becomes +821012345678
    • Message: sms_internal_alerts. A trial account sends only Twilio’s sample texts; this name picks the alert.
  4. Click Execute workflow, once.

Within a minute your phone shows Twilio’s sample alert, in Korea marked [국제발신]: Alert: System downtime detected. … Well done: your n8n can now reach a phone. Your own text needs a paid Twilio account. SOLAPI seems to give you more freedom in its trial: from what it writes, the points you get at sign-up already send your own text, from your own number. For this, you need a Korean phone number in your own name.
